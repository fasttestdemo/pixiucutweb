Frequently asked questions

Is PixiuCut free?

The core features are free — download and try local editing. The full version is unlocked with a license code; see the pricing page.

Is my footage uploaded to the cloud?

No. Decoding, composing and export all happen on your machine — your footage never leaves your computer.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows x64 is the primary platform today. macOS and Linux builds are planned.

Which aspect ratios are supported?

Portrait-first with a 9:16 default, plus 16:9, 1:1, 4:3 and more.

Can I extend it?

Yes. Extend features with plugins, with local install and management — see the plugin dev guide.
